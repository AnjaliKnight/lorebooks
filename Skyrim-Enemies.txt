# ENEMIES OF SKYRIM

Skyrim is a harsh land. The Civil War has made roads less safe, weakened local authority, and driven many desperate people toward crime, but even in peaceful times the wilderness has never been truly safe.

Danger comes in many forms. Some enemies are ordinary people who have chosen violence. Others are animals that see travelers as prey or threats. The most dangerous are creatures touched by magic, curses, or forces older than human civilization.

Not every creature listed here is inherently evil. A wolf defending its territory, a troll guarding its cave, or a spriggan protecting an ancient forest is not necessarily malicious. Nevertheless, all of them can become deadly threats to travelers, hunters, farmers, soldiers, and settlements.

---

## PART I: HUMANS

Human enemies are often the most unpredictable. Unlike beasts and monsters, they understand roads, settlements, politics, trade routes, and human weaknesses.

Many are former soldiers, criminals, desperate refugees, religious fanatics, or people who have simply discovered that violence is profitable.

The Civil War has made the problem considerably worse.

### Bandits

Bandits are among the most common threats on Skyrim's roads.

They usually operate in small groups from abandoned forts, caves, old watchtowers, mines, ruined settlements, or hidden camps in the wilderness. Some are little more than starving criminals. Others are organized groups with experienced fighters, scouts, archers, and leaders.

Bandits survive by robbing travelers, merchants, hunters, farmers, and isolated settlements.

They commonly use bows, hunting knives, swords, axes, maces, and whatever armor they can acquire.

The Civil War has created ideal conditions for banditry. Abandoned military positions, reduced patrols, disrupted trade, and displaced civilians have given criminal groups more opportunities.

Not every bandit is a hardened killer. Some joined a gang because they were desperate. Others have become professional criminals who view robbery and murder simply as a means of survival.

### Marauders

Marauders are more organized and aggressive than ordinary bandits.

They are often former soldiers, mercenaries, raiders, or experienced criminals who operate under a strong leader. Marauder groups may occupy fortified positions and possess better weapons and armor than common bandits.

Some groups specialize in attacking caravans or travelers. Others raid villages, seize livestock, extort settlements, or sell stolen goods.

During the Civil War, marauders can also include deserters and former soldiers who have abandoned their allegiance and turned to organized crime.

### Highwaymen and Muggers

Not every criminal maintains a permanent camp.

Highwaymen and muggers usually operate along roads, bridges, mountain passes, and isolated trails. They rely on surprise and intimidation rather than prolonged battles.

A traveler may be surrounded by several men who demand money, weapons, food, or valuables.

Some robbers are willing to let victims go after taking their belongings. Others kill witnesses.

### Thieves

Thieves usually avoid unnecessary violence, preferring stealth, deception, burglary, pickpocketing, and smuggling.

They are particularly common around larger settlements such as Riften, Whiterun, Solitude, Windhelm, and Markarth.

Some operate independently. Others belong to organized criminal networks.

Thieves are dangerous in a different way from bandits. They can enter homes, gather information, follow people without being noticed, and disappear into populated areas.

### Assassins

Assassins are professional killers hired to eliminate specific targets.

Unlike bandits, they rarely attack randomly. Their strength comes from preparation, secrecy, and choosing the right moment.

Assassins may work for criminal organizations, wealthy individuals, political factions, cults, or secretive groups.

The Dark Brotherhood is the most infamous organization associated with professional assassination, although independent killers and other organizations also exist.

The Civil War creates additional opportunities for assassination because nobles, officers, merchants, spies, and political figures all have enemies.

### Deserters

The Civil War has produced another dangerous category of human enemy: deserters.

Some soldiers abandon their posts because they are afraid, exhausted, wounded, disillusioned, or simply want to survive.

A few disappear into civilian life.

Others form armed groups and become little different from bandits.

Deserters can be particularly dangerous because they possess military training and knowledge of patrol routes, fortifications, weapons, and battlefield tactics.

### Mercenaries

Mercenaries fight for payment rather than political loyalty.

Many are perfectly legitimate sellswords who protect merchants, caravans, estates, or settlements.

Others are little more than armed criminals.

During the Civil War, mercenaries may be hired to protect property, hunt fugitives, intimidate rivals, escort important people, or perform unofficial work that governments do not want associated with their own soldiers.

### Necromancers

Necromancers practice forbidden forms of magic involving the dead, souls, corpses, and the manipulation of life.

They are often found in abandoned tombs, caves, ruins, isolated towers, and ancient Nordic burial sites.

Necromancers may raise corpses as undead servants, summon magical creatures, drain the life from living beings, or experiment upon bodies and souls.

Their motivations vary.

Some seek immortality. Others pursue knowledge, power, revenge, or simple curiosity.

Whatever their reason, most civilized people consider necromancy dangerous and abhorrent.

### Cultists

Cultists are devotees of forbidden gods, Daedra, ancient powers, or obscure religious movements.

Their beliefs vary enormously.

Some worship Daedric Princes. Others follow ancient traditions that have survived in secret. Some groups conduct sacrifices, summon supernatural entities, or attempt to bring their chosen deity or master into the mortal world.

Cultists are particularly dangerous because they may disguise themselves as ordinary citizens until their activities are uncovered.

### Forsworn

The Forsworn are a militant Reachfolk movement centered primarily around the Reach.

They reject Imperial and Nord control of the Reach and seek to reclaim the territory for their own people.

Forsworn fighters are commonly encountered in remote forts, caves, ruins, and wilderness settlements.

They use traditional Reachfolk weapons and armor and often employ hedge magic, traps, poisons, and supernatural allies.

Not every Reachfolk person is Forsworn, and the distinction between ordinary Reachfolk and Forsworn should not be assumed.

### Thalmor Agents and Justiciars

Thalmor agents are not ordinary criminals, but they can become enemies of individuals who interfere with Thalmor interests.

Justiciars travel Skyrim enforcing the restrictions imposed by the White-Gold Concordat, particularly the prohibition on Talos worship.

They may investigate suspected heresy, arrest individuals, interrogate prisoners, and use force when necessary.

Their presence is especially unpopular among many Nords.

During the Civil War, their activities become even more politically sensitive because both Imperial and Stormcloak sympathizers may view them with suspicion.

### Smugglers and Criminal Networks

Smugglers transport stolen goods, contraband, weapons, illegal substances, fugitives, and other valuable commodities.

They are particularly active around ports, major roads, borders, mines, and cities.

Unlike bandits, smugglers usually depend upon secrecy and connections rather than violence.

However, anyone who threatens their business can quickly discover that smugglers are capable of hiring very violent people.

# PART II: BEASTS

The wilderness of Skyrim is filled with dangerous wildlife.

Most beasts are not evil. They hunt because they need food, defend territory, protect their young, or respond to threats.

For an unprepared traveler, however, the distinction between an evil creature and a hungry predator offers little comfort.

### Wolves

Wolves are among the most common predators in Skyrim.

They hunt individually or in packs and commonly attack deer, livestock, and travelers.

A lone wolf is rarely a serious threat to an armed adult.

A pack is considerably more dangerous.

Wolves are most common in forests, mountains, and open wilderness.

### Bears

Bears are powerful territorial predators.

They normally avoid people when possible, but they can become extremely aggressive when surprised, cornered, or defending their young.

A bear's strength allows it to kill an unarmored person very quickly.

Hunters generally give known bear territories a wide berth.

### Sabre Cats

Sabre cats are large feline predators with powerful jaws, claws, and elongated canine teeth.

They are considerably more dangerous than ordinary wolves.

They commonly inhabit forests, tundra, and mountainous regions.

Sabre cats are excellent hunters and can remain hidden until they are close enough to attack.

Snowy regions are home to pale variants that are particularly difficult to spot against the landscape.

### Mammoths

Mammoths are enormous herd animals associated particularly with Skyrim's tundra.

They are normally peaceful when left alone.

The danger comes from their enormous size, strength, and defensive behavior.

Attacking a mammoth is extremely dangerous and usually foolish unless the hunter is experienced and properly equipped.

Mammoth herds are commonly associated with giant camps.

### Giants

Giants should be included among Skyrim's dangerous wildlife.

Although intelligent enough to communicate and maintain camps, giants generally keep to themselves.

They herd mammoths, establish camps, and defend their territory aggressively.

They are not mindless monsters and should not be treated as such.

A giant can, however, kill a human with a single powerful blow.

Approaching a giant camp without understanding the danger is one of the easiest ways for an inexperienced traveler to die.

### Frostbite Spiders

Frostbite spiders are large predatory spiders found throughout Skyrim.

They live in caves, ruins, abandoned structures, forests, and underground passages.

Their venom can incapacitate or kill an unprotected person.

Some are relatively small, while others grow to enormous sizes.

Their ability to hide in dark environments makes them particularly dangerous to travelers exploring ruins and caves.

### Snakes

Several species of snake inhabit Skyrim.

Most are not particularly dangerous to experienced travelers, but venomous varieties can cause serious injury.

Snakes are generally more dangerous because they are difficult to see than because they actively hunt humans.

### Scorpions

Scorpions are uncommon in Skyrim compared with warmer regions of Tamriel, but isolated varieties can survive in suitable environments, particularly underground or unusually warm areas.

Their claws and venom make them dangerous to inexperienced travelers.

### Mudcrabs

Mudcrabs are common around Skyrim's rivers, lakes, marshes, and coastlines.

Most are small and harmless.

Larger specimens can be surprisingly aggressive and possess strong claws capable of causing painful injuries.

They are more nuisance than serious threat, but a large group can still be dangerous.

### Slaughterfish

Slaughterfish inhabit Skyrim's rivers, lakes, and coastal waters.

They are aggressive aquatic predators and can attack swimmers or people attempting to cross waterways.

Their presence makes swimming across unfamiliar waters considerably more dangerous than it appears.

### Horkers

Horkers are large marine mammals commonly found along Skyrim's northern coast.

They are normally peaceful but can become aggressive when threatened.

Their size and tusks make them dangerous at close range.

# PART III: MONSTERS

Monsters are creatures that cannot be explained simply as ordinary wildlife.

Some are magical beings. Some are humans transformed by curses. Others are ancient creatures that belong to magical ecosystems older than modern civilization.

They are generally far more dangerous than ordinary animals.

### Werewolves

Werewolves are humans afflicted with lycanthropy, a supernatural condition commonly associated with Hircine.

A werewolf can transform from human form into a large, powerful beast.

In this form, they possess extraordinary strength, speed, endurance, and regenerative abilities.

Werewolves retain intelligence and can be extremely dangerous opponents.

Not every werewolf is inherently evil. Some willingly embrace the curse, while others may seek to control or remove it.

### Vampires

Vampires are undead beings sustained by consuming the blood of the living.

They possess supernatural strength, speed, longevity, and magical abilities.

Some vampires hide within ordinary society and maintain convincing human identities.

Others live in isolated lairs and hunt openly.

Vampirism can exist in different forms, with some bloodlines possessing abilities far beyond those of ordinary vampires.

### Atronachs

Atronachs are magical beings formed from elemental forces.

The most commonly encountered varieties are Flame, Frost, and Storm Atronachs.

They are not ordinary living creatures.

Their bodies are composed of magical elemental energy and they can survive injuries that would kill a normal animal or person.

Atronachs may be summoned by mages or encountered as independent magical entities.

### Spriggans

Spriggans are mysterious nature spirits associated with forests and ancient places.

They resemble humanoid forms made from wood, roots, leaves, and living vegetation.

Spriggans are strongly connected to the natural world and can command or influence animals.

They are often hostile toward intruders who disturb their territory.

Their regenerative abilities make them difficult opponents.

### Wisps

Wisps are strange magical entities that appear as floating balls of light.

They are often encountered in remote wilderness, ruins, and places saturated with magic.

Despite their small appearance, wisps can be dangerous because they possess magical abilities and are difficult to fight using ordinary weapons.

### Wisp Mothers

Wisp Mothers are more powerful forms of wisps.

They are considerably more dangerous than ordinary wisps and can use powerful magical attacks.

They should never be mistaken for harmless lights or wandering spirits.

### Trolls

Trolls are large, aggressive creatures found throughout Skyrim.

They possess enormous physical strength and remarkable regenerative abilities.

Most trolls are territorial and hostile.

Frost trolls are particularly dangerous in Skyrim's colder regions.

Trolls can recover from wounds that would incapacitate a human, making fire especially useful when fighting them.

### Frost Trolls

Frost trolls are adapted to Skyrim's cold northern environment.

They are stronger and better suited to the frozen wilderness than ordinary trolls.

They are frequently encountered in mountainous regions, caves, and remote passes.

### Draugr

Draugr are undead inhabitants of ancient Nordic tombs.

They are the remains of ancient warriors, priests, and other individuals who were buried according to old Nordic traditions.

Some are little more than animated corpses.

Others retain considerable strength, intelligence, weapons, and magical abilities.

Powerful Draugr can use the Thu'um.

They are among the most dangerous enemies found within ancient Nordic ruins.

### Deathlords and Greater Draugr

Some Draugr are vastly more powerful than ordinary undead.

These ancient warriors may possess exceptional physical strength, powerful weapons, and knowledge of the Voice.

They often guard important tombs and ancient burial sites.

### Skeletons

Skeletons are undead remains animated through necromantic or magical forces.

They are weaker than many other undead creatures but can appear in large numbers.

They are commonly found in tombs, ruins, crypts, and places associated with necromancy.

### Ghosts and Restless Spirits

Not every dead person passes peacefully into whatever afterlife awaits them.

Some spirits remain bound to the mortal world because of curses, unfinished business, magical interference, or the nature of their death.

Ghosts can be difficult to fight because ordinary physical weapons may have limited effectiveness against them.

### Falmer

The Falmer are the descendants of the ancient Snow Elves who were driven underground centuries ago.

Generations spent beneath Skyrim changed them physically and culturally.

They now inhabit vast underground settlements and ruins, particularly beneath Skyrim's mountains.

Falmer are intelligent, organized, and extremely dangerous.

They use weapons, poisons, traps, trained creatures, and ambush tactics.

They should not be confused with ordinary monsters.

They are a distinct people with their own society, history, and culture.

### Chaurus

Chaurus are large subterranean arthropods commonly associated with Falmer settlements.

They possess powerful mandibles and venomous attacks.

Chaurus larvae are smaller but can still be dangerous.

Their presence is often an indication that Falmer are nearby.

### Hagravens

Hagravens are monstrous humanoid witches created through dark magical transformation.

They possess powerful magical abilities and often live in isolated camps, caves, towers, or ancient ruins.

They are associated particularly with forbidden magic, nature worship, and certain Reachfolk traditions.

Hagravens are intelligent and capable of planning, making them more dangerous than ordinary beasts.

### Ice Wraiths

Ice Wraiths are magical creatures associated with cold and snow.

They resemble serpentine forms made from ice and magical energy.

They move rapidly and can be difficult to hit.

They are commonly encountered in Skyrim's northern and mountainous regions.

### Giants' Mammoth Guardians and Other Magical Creatures

Skyrim's wilderness also contains numerous lesser magical creatures and unusual beings that do not fit neatly into a single category.

Some are created by magic.

Others are ancient species whose origins are poorly understood.

Travelers should never assume that a creature is harmless simply because they have never encountered it before.

## GENERAL THREAT

The greatest danger in Skyrim is not any single enemy.

It is the combination of dangers.

A traveler might leave a city to avoid political trouble and encounter bandits on the road. The bandits may be driven from their camp by soldiers and forced into the wilderness, where they begin robbing travelers more aggressively. A fleeing traveler may take shelter in a cave and discover that it belongs to a necromancer, Falmer, troll, or pack of frostbite spiders.

The Civil War makes these encounters more common.

Military patrols are stretched thin. Trade routes are disrupted. Criminals exploit the chaos. Refugees move through dangerous territory. Abandoned forts and watchposts become hideouts.

And beyond all of this, the return of dragons introduces a threat that few people are prepared to understand.

Skyrim has always been dangerous.

The difference now is that many of the forces keeping that danger contained are beginning to fail.
